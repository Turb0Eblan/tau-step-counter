About Tau Step Counter
Tau Step Counter
Version 2.0
Free software, MIT license.

Counts photobleaching steps in single-molecule fluorescence traces, to tell monomers from dimers and bigger oligomers.

Each trace is smoothed with total variation denoising (Chambolle, 2004), the same filter as skimage.restoration.denoise_tv_chambolle.

Everything runs on your computer. Files are never uploaded anywhere.
